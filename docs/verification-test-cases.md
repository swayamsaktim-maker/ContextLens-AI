# Verification regression cases

Use these after configuring Google Search and Fact Check keys.

| Claim | Expected | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Narendra Modi is the Prime Minister of India. | VERIFIED | Official PM India evidence supports it. |
| Trump is President of India. | FALSE | Official White House evidence identifies Trump as US president, contradicting the proposition. |
| The moon is made of cheese. | FALSE | NASA evidence describes the Moon as a rocky body; cheese is contradicted. |
| Scientists confirmed that drinking lemon water cures cancer. | FALSE | Medical/scientific evidence should contradict the cure proposition. |
| The Moon has an unknown mineral discovered tomorrow. | UNVERIFIED | Future/novel proposition should not be guessed without evidence. |

A claim should never become `FALSE` solely because a Google Fact Check search returned zero records.
